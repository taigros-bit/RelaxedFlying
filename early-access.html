Quick answers

Is it free?
Yes. The seat map at relaxedflying.app is completely free, no account needed. AI predictions and alerts are coming soon — waitlist members get first access.
When does the full app launch?
We're targeting August 2026. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know.
How does the turbulence data work?
We pull live PIREPs, METAR and weather forecast data for your specific route, then map expected turbulence intensity onto each seat position based on aircraft physics.
Which airlines and aircraft are supported?
We support 2,000+ airports and all major commercial aircraft types. Enter your flight number and we handle the rest.
